# The Acquisition Files

A study game for FAC-COR, FAC-P/PM, and the CFCM exam. Open `index.html` in a browser, or play the deployed site.

The game opens on Home and leads you through all 539 files as a series of workdays at a harbor station. Each workday brings a few new rules, then an inbox of documents to inspect and stamp on a 9-to-5 clock, with citations, audit findings that come back later, and an end-of-day report. The model is Papers, Please, with a Fallout Shelter-style station that your work keeps running. Fifteen chapters follow an acquisition from the ground rules to closeout, and each one ends with a finale that opens the next.

From Day 2, the desk also brings visitors at the window (choices with consequences that can come back days later), paperwork checks (match an invoice against the contract and receiving report, then pay, return, or hold), and station crises that your staff have to handle. A story, the Brightwater file, runs through all 15 chapters, with one clue for each finale you pass. Achievements, streaks, and optional sound round it out.

Everything runs as one campaign. Each chapter is workdays, then required incidents (all 12 story cases and all 12 field files, plus two rule dockets), then the finale, then a harbor rebuild: the original harbor story's 12 sites, plus three landmarks. Board reviews after chapters 5, 10, and 15 need 70% to continue. Trust and mission respond to how carefully you work: high trust pays a daily bonus, low trust makes crises likelier, and mission sets how much your staffed rooms produce. Tap any meter in the top bar to see what it's doing.

The look is a harbor at night: a HUD with your funds, trust, mission, and streak; a Home screen where the 15 chapters fly as maritime signal flags A to O; a booth-and-desk workday with typed records, carbon-copy invoices, and rubber-stamp ink; and a shift report with a stamped grade. The fonts are Big Shoulders Stencil, Barlow Condensed, and Courier Prime (SIL Open Font License), embedded so the game works offline.

Every catalogued objective moves through the same five stages: Watch, Work together, Independent, Connect, and Lead. One cast of characters keeps the same job titles across the Records Hall files, the field files, the story cases, and the station.

All scenarios, names, records, and figures are original and fictional. This is practice material, not certification, approved training hours, or NCMA/FAI material. Progress saves in the browser you play in; use Menu → Export and Import to move it between devices.
